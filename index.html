Luzon

Luzon sits at the northern end of the Philippines, and is the country’s largest and most populous island. It’s known for its mountains, beaches and coral reefs, and is home to Manila, the national capital. Set on a deep bay with famed sunsets, the city has many Spanish-colonial landmarks, national memorials and monuments, a centuries-old Chinatown and a diversity of museums.

Visayas

Visayas is one of the three main island groups in the Philippines. It is located in the central part of the country and is known for its beautiful beaches, islands, mountains, rich culture, and historical places. The people in Visayas speak different languages such as Cebuano, Hiligaynon, Waray, and Kinaray-a. It is also known for festivals like Sinulog, Ati-Atihan, and Dinagyang.

Mindanao

Mindanao is one of the three main island groups in the Philippines. It is located in the southern part of the country and is known for its beautiful mountains, beaches, rivers, and rich natural resources. It has diverse cultures and languages, including Cebuano, Maranao, Tausug, and Maguindanaon. Mindanao is also known for places such as Davao, Siargao, Camiguin, and Lake Lanao.

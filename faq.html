Questions from the bus stop

Start here before you plan a Philippine adventure.

These answers are written for beginner travelers and student researchers. Click a question to open the note.

It is a school practice travelogue about discovering the Philippines. “Travel ” means journey, and our pages collect short guides, photos, and personal observations.
Our first set includes Boracay, Palawan, the Banaue Rice Terraces, Cebu, Baguio, and Siargao. A future class can add more places using the same simple page structure.
Look up current weather, transport schedules, entrance rules, local advisories, and accommodation details. Conditions and prices change, so do not use a school practice page as your only source.
Follow community rules, ask before taking photographs of people, reduce single-use plastic, stay on marked paths, and support local businesses when you can.
The links are starter images for practice. Students should replace them with their own photos or images they are allowed to use, then add a clear credit near the photo.

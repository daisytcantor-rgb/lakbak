LUZON FAMOUS FOOD

Sisig

Sisig, a famous Kapampangan dish from Pampanga, is known for its crispy texture, savory flavor, and sizzling hot serving. It is a beloved Filipino dish that showcases the rich food culture of Pampanga.

A useful habit

Leave space in the itinerary.

Travel days can change because of weather, ferry schedules, or a conversation that runs long. Our imaginary packing list includes water, sun protection, a reusable bag, and enough time to notice where we are.

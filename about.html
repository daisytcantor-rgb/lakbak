About our notebook

Three classmates, one map, and a lot of questions.

Lakbay Notes is our friendly practice project about exploring the Philippines with open eyes and a respectful sense of adventure.

Why we made this

Travel is more than just a destination.

We created this travelogue to share places through the eyes of students not like a formal brochure, but through stories, experiences, discoveries, and the little moments that make a trip memorable.

Every guide on this site is a starting point. Before a real trip, check current local rules, weather, transport schedules, and advice from the communities who live there.
